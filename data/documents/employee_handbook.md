# Brightpath Solutions — Employee Handbook

Effective date: 1 April 2026. This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Brightpath Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (the “Company”). For questions, email hr@brightpath.example.

## Working hours

Core office hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 18:30 IST, with a 45-minute lunch break. Teams may agree a flexible start between 08:30 and 10:30 as long as eight working hours are completed. Weekend work is not expected except for on-call rotations described in the engineering onboarding guide.

## Hybrid work and WFH

Brightpath is a hybrid company. Employees may work from home **two days per week**. The default in-office days are Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. Your manager can approve a different split if the team is covered. Fully remote work is available only for roles tagged “remote-eligible” on the offer letter.

When working from home you must be reachable on Slack during core hours and join the daily standup on camera unless traveling.

## Leave policy

Annual leave entitlements for full-time employees:

- **Casual leave:** 12 days per calendar year
- **Sick leave:** 10 days per calendar year
- **Earned / privilege leave:** 18 days per calendar year, accrued monthly
- **Parental leave:** 26 weeks for the primary caregiver; 2 weeks for the secondary caregiver
- **Bereavement leave:** 5 days

Casual leave cannot be taken for more than 3 consecutive days without manager approval. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate. Unused casual and sick leave expire on 31 December and do not carry forward. Up to 10 unused earned-leave days may be carried into the next year.

Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their weekly hours.

## Public holidays

Brightpath observes 12 public holidays each year. The official list is published by HR every December. In 2026 the company-wide holidays include Republic Day, Holi, Good Friday, Independence Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali (2 days), Christmas, and four floating holidays chosen by the employee from the regional calendar.

If a holiday falls on a weekend, HR will announce a substitute weekday off. There is no extra pay for working a holiday unless you are on an approved on-call shift.

## Probation and notice

New hires are on probation for **90 days**. During probation, either party may end employment with 7 days’ notice. After confirmation, the notice period is 30 days for individual contributors and 60 days for managers (L5 and above).

## Expenses and travel

Local travel for client meetings is reimbursed against receipts through the Zoho Expense portal within 14 days. Meal allowance while traveling is ₹800 per meal, up to two meals per day. Flights under 3 hours are economy class; longer flights may be premium economy with VP approval.

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are prohibited. Report concerns to hr@brightpath.example or use the anonymous ethics form on the intranet. Gifts from vendors above ₹2,000 must be declared to Finance.

## Office locations

Headquarters is at WeWork Galaxy, Residency Road, Bengaluru. A satellite office operates in Pune (Koregaon Park). Both offices provide hot desks, meeting rooms, and visitor Wi-Fi. Badge access is required after 19:00.
